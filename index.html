Understanding your result

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 falls in the healthy weight range. Staying there can lower your risk of problems such as obesity and type 2 diabetes. Build meals around fruit and vegetables with less sugar and fat, and aim for about 30 minutes of movement on five days each week.

When BMI falls short

BMI is a handy quick check, but it does not fit everyone. Some groups should read their result with care, and sometimes another measure tells a better story.

wc

Gender

Boys and girls grow and store body fat differently as they age, so a child’s age and gender must be considered when reading their BMI.

cake

Age

Older adults tend to gain body fat and lose muscle, which can make BMI underestimate how much body fat they carry.

fitness_center

Muscle

BMI cannot tell muscle from fat, so very muscular people may be labelled overweight or obese.

pregnant_woman

Pregnancy

Weight rises during pregnancy to support the baby. A healthy BMI before pregnancy helps reduce health risks for mother and child.

groups

Race

Some Black and Asian groups can face health risks at a lower BMI. Speak to your GP or practice nurse to learn more.
